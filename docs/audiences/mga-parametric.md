# For MGAs and parametric insurance

See [SAMPLE_DATA.md](../SAMPLE_DATA.md) for response shapes.

- **ML Storm Forecast by Address** returns address-level records with a forecast time, storm type, risk label, structure replacement value and estimated people, across 10 perils and windows from 24 hours to 10 days. A usable input when you design triggers and exposure views.
- **Storm History by Address** gives dated reported and radar-detected events back to 2003 for back-testing a trigger idea at the address.
- Self-serve, pay-per-address pricing (/bin/zsh.05) means a small MGA can integrate without an enterprise sales cycle.

None of this is a damage certification or a property inspection. A reported or forecast event is a signal worth following up on, not proof of loss. Forecasts describe what could happen, not what will happen.
