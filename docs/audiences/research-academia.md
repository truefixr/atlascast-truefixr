# For research and academia

See [SAMPLE_DATA.md](../SAMPLE_DATA.md) for response shapes.

- Nationwide, address-level: **Storm history near an address** (since 2003), **Recent storms near addresses** (the last 365 days) and the **AtlasCast address forecast** (24 hours to 10 days ahead) in one API. Most public datasets are one or the other, or county-level.
- Every request returns its exact price first, and `coverage` costs $0.0002, so you can scope a study for pennies.
- Every paid answer is a dated JSON document, so a pull can be kept and cited as read.

None of this is a damage certification or a property inspection. A reported or forecast event is a signal worth following up on, not proof of loss. Forecasts describe what could happen, not what will happen.
