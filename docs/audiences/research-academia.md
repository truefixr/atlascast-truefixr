# For research and academia

See [SAMPLE_DATA.md](../SAMPLE_DATA.md) for response shapes.

- Nationwide, address-level: **Storm History by Address: Reported and Radar-Detected Events Since 2003**, **Past Storm Leads by Address**, and **ML Storm Forecast by Address** in one API. Most public datasets are one or the other, or county-level.
- Free previews and always-free `coverage` and `daily` datasets let you scope a study before spending anything.
- Every paid response carries a `reference_id`, so pulls are citable and reproducible.

None of this is a damage certification or a property inspection. A reported or forecast event is a signal worth following up on, not proof of loss. Forecasts describe what could happen, not what will happen.
